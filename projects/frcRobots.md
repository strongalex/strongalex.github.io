<p class="meta"><em>2019-Present</em></p>
<p class="lead">FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is a robotics competition for high school students. Two alliances of three robots, each weighing around 150 lb, compete against each other, and the game is different every year.</p>

<p>Throughout college, I have helped out FRC 190 through mentoring the students on the team. I help with match strategy and design of the robotic systems. Usually I am leading a group of students through the design process, designing, providing feedback, and distributing tasks. I&rsquo;ve also gotten a lot of experience on the manufacturing side of things as we make lots of our parts in house.</p>

<p>Throughout high school, I worked on the scouting/strategy website and robot design for FRC 195.</p>
