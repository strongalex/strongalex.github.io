    <h1 class="subtitle">FRC Team 190</h1>
    <p class="meta"><em>2026 season</em></p>

    <p class="lead">Our first robot for the 2026 FRC game, "REBUILT."</p>
    <p>Its primary goals were to shoot yellow foam "Fuel" balls into an alliance Hub at the center of our side of the field, and to climb the rungs of a ladder-shaped Tower at the end of the match. The robot advanced far into the district championship and qualified the team for the world championships, where it also performed well. My main contributions were to the shooter subsystem. The robot was originally designed in Onshape.</p>

<div class="split split--stack">
  <div class="split__text">
    <h2 id="custom-prototype">Custom prototype</h2>
    <p>The custom prototype had three adjustments:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>An adjustable hood angle</li>
      <li>An optional divider</li>
      <li>Adjustable compression, set by changing the back piece</li>
    </ul>
    <p>It was built mainly from CNC-cut plywood and 3D printed parts so we could iterate quickly. We used it to inform the amount of compression and the hood angle the real version needed.</p>
